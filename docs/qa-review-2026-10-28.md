# QA review, 28 Oct 2026

Every page of the app (163 routes, plus each request type) was tested by hand in the browser in
mock mode: forms submitted empty, with invalid values and with valid ones, every row action,
filter, tab, dialog and export tried, results followed into the screens they feed, then each page
checked at 375 px and in dark mode. Three testers covered People & Time, Requests / Payroll /
Benefits, and Projects / Settings / the new modules. The fixes below were made the same day and
re-tested; what is still open is listed after them.

## Fixed

**Across the app**

- The mock API kept dropping (every 1–3 minutes every call answered 502). The app's recovery
  only reacted to 404 or HTML answers; it now also treats the dev proxy's empty 502 / 503 / 504
  as "the mock missed this" and restarts it (`src/lib/axios.ts`).
- Focus fell to the page after closing any dialog. Dialogs and confirms now give focus back to
  the control that opened them, also when they open from a row ⋮ menu
  (`src/components/ui/return-focus.ts`).
- The searchable pickers (employees, pay items…) work from the keyboard: arrow keys, Enter, a
  "Searching…" line, and a proper search-box name.
- CSV exports keep typed formulas (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`) as text and start with a UTF-8 marker, so
  Excel shows names correctly.
- Pages no longer scroll sideways at 1024 px or on phones (Project Management, Manpower
  Planning, Recruitment and others: screen-reader text escaped its scroll box).
- Project Management's workspace pages have a dark mode.
- The sidebar's group arrows all show open / closed; the 404 page sits inside the app with its
  own title; notifications open their page; dashboard tiles link to their lists.

**Data that did not add up**

- Leaves and Requests → Leave now share one set of requests and balances: an applied leave
  shows in both, approving moves the balance, and holidays are not counted.
- Promotion could not be created (its Grade list was empty); fixed, and the current salary now
  shows on Create.
- Payroll: one person on one payroll a month; no negative pay lines, net pay or salaries; times in
  local time; My Pay Slips shows only released slips; approved vouchers cannot be deleted;
  settings are checked instead of silently changed; bank details are validated.
- The Benefits Employees tab, Leaves and the Leave mock use the shared employee list.
- Recruitment: hired people get a unique employee code; offers and interviews cannot be in the
  past; an expired offer shows Expired.
- Landing pages count and link correctly (Final Settlement, End of Employment, Resignations).
- Attendance: approved overtime shows on the time sheet, holidays clear the scheduling board,
  machine and geo-fence names are no longer doubled.

**Forms and feedback**

- Many forms now show every error inline at once, before any "Are you sure?" (Attendance
  holidays and rest days, Payroll settings, Expense approval, Rehire, Request Flows…).
- Discard-changes prompts on Talent Management dialogs, Manpower Planning edit, General
  Settings Cancel and the off-boarding checklist.
- Actions the server would refuse are hidden or disabled with the reason (delete of used assets
  or approved clearances, Launch with no participants, Complete while items wait, delete of an
  elected benefit plan).
- Pickers offer only valid choices (dates, branches of a location, people not already chosen).
- Wording, plurals and labels across the list in each module's README section.

### To confirm with the backend

- Permission codes for Recruitment, Performance and Custom Form are assumed
  (`recruitment.<screen>.<action>`, …). `/api/access/permissions` lists the real ones; User
  Group Permissions now shows that list, so the codes can be checked there against the real API.
- Recruitment status values for openings, interviews and offers are not in the OpenAPI.
- What a "Public" workspace means (assumed: everyone in the organisation can see it).
- Whether an offer must expire before the joining date (kept as built).
- Talent Management uses `EMP-0001` codes, every other module `Id-001`: one format is needed.

### Suggestions for the best experience, most valuable first

1. **One date, time and money format** everywhere, following General Settings' Date Format.
   Today at least eight date styles and three money styles appear.
2. **One dialog standard**: button order (Cancel then the action), neutral Cancel colour, the same
   words (Save, not Save Changes / Add), required marks on every required field, confirms that
   name the record ("Delete Sara Khan's request?") instead of "Perform this action?".
3. **Errors are not empty lists**: several lists still say "No … match" when loading failed; show
   "Could not load…" with Try again everywhere (the Approve dialog already does).
4. **Done 29 Oct 2026:** System Users, User Group Permissions and Biometric Devices are built
   on their APIs (README sections of those names), with Change Password in the account menu.
5. **Validate before confirming** in the remaining forms, and limit every date picker to valid
   days.
6. **Deep links**: Talent Management's Related links (Transfer, Salary Revision, Employee
   Accounts, Pay Slips, Loans) should open filtered to the person; those pages need an employee
   filter in their URL.
7. **Undo** for quick destructive actions (deactivate an employee, delete a row) instead of, or
   besides, a confirm.
8. **Shared employee data** in the mocks: one directory for codes, departments and managers
   (Leaves and Benefits now use it; Talent Management still has its own codes).
9. **Merge the two Scheduling pages** (`/scheduling` and Attendance → Scheduling).
10. **Calendar**: mark holidays and weekends in the date picker (Leaves already warns about
    them in the form).
